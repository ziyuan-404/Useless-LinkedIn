# Application Rules Example

This is a fake demo. Replace all details before real use.

## Mode

Selected mode: Volume

Use stable resume variants for normal applications. Promote high-fit PM or Growth PM roles to Precision when they are worth tailoring.

## Prioritize

Apply quickly when a job matches:

- Role families: Product Manager, Growth PM, Product Analyst.
- Titles: Product Manager, Associate Product Manager, Growth Product Manager, Product Analyst.
- Level: entry to mid-level; senior only if years requirement is flexible.
- Freshness: posted in the last 24 hours first, then 48 hours.
- Locations: New York, Remote US, San Francisco Bay Area.
- Company types: AI tools, B2B SaaS, data products, developer tools.
- Form length: LinkedIn Easy Apply or short ATS forms first.

## Consider

Review before applying when:

- Senior PM asks for 5+ years but role content is highly relevant.
- Location is hybrid outside target cities.
- Compensation is not listed.
- The role seems like product marketing or GTM but has strong product overlap.

## Skip

Default skip when:

- Title includes Staff, Principal, Director, Head, VP.
- Required experience is 7+ years.
- Role is commission-only sales.
- Location is onsite outside target cities.
- Employer says no sponsorship and the real candidate requires it.
- The form requires a video, long writing sample, or references before interview.
- Duplicate, closed, or already applied.

## Hand Off to User

Stop and ask when:

- Work authorization wording differs from the profile.
- CAPTCHA, Cloudflare, login, or 2FA appears.
- Resume upload cannot be verified.
- A form requires compensation below or above the stored range.
- A high-value tailored resume is ready for review.
